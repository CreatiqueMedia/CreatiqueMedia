# CreatiqueMedia LLC

**Veteran-owned apps, websites, and digital media.**

I'm Ron Ratzlaff, founder of CreatiqueMedia LLC in Edmond, Oklahoma. My background includes ten years in U.S. Air Force cyber operations and more than twenty years in IT, systems engineering, and software development across healthcare, military, and government environments.

Today I build useful, engaging apps and help businesses improve their online presence through websites, video, and digital media.
